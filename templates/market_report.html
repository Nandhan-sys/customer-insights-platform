Slide 01 / 05 The Business Problem

Why Treating All Customers the Same Wastes Money

Most businesses make the mistake of giving the same generic discount coupon to everyone. But customers are completely different: some buy at full price, while others only visit for food or movies.

The Common Mistake

Giving coupons to people who would already buy at full price wastes 15% of company profit for no reason.

The Solution

Group customers by their real shopping habits so you know who needs a deal and who prefers VIP perks.

Our Goal

Use AI to automatically find customer types and build an easy, codeless website for the marketing team.

Slide 04 / 05 Business Advice for Management

3 Smart Business Decisions to Make More Money

Decision 1
Stop Discounting Fashion Shoppers

Group 0 visits ~15 times a month and rarely uses discounts. Stop giving them coupons—they happily pay full price. Offer VIP styling and early previews instead.

Decision 2
Dinner Bundles for Big Spenders

Group 1 spends the most ($3,561/mo) and loves good deals. Offer high-spend rewards (like 'Spend $150 on dinner, get a $30 voucher') to encourage bigger checks.

Decision 3
Monetize Long Hangout Times

Group 2 hangs out for over 2 hours for movies and games. Send them mobile flash deals (e.g. 20% off popcorn or retail) while they wait for their movie to start.
