Simple Action Guide for Your Team

What Marketing, Sales, and Customer Service teams should do for each group

Team Function Regular Fashion Shoppers (Group 0) Big Spenders on Dining (Group 1) Movie & Hangout Visitors (Group 2)
Finding New Leads Target style-conscious people on Instagram. Show off new apparel collections and fresh arrivals. Target working professionals and local food lovers on Google & LinkedIn. Highlight business lunch and dinner spots. Target college students and families on social media. Promote upcoming movies, gaming arcades, and weekend events.
Deals & Discounts Do not give discounts! They happily buy at regular price. Offer free styling advice or early access instead. Use spend bundles: 'Spend $150 on dinner, get a $30 gift card.' This encourages larger family and business orders. Use combo vouchers: 'Buy 1 Movie Ticket, get 20% off popcorn, snacks, or arcade tokens.'
Keeping Them Happy Create an exclusive VIP club with private lounge access and early invitations to seasonal launches. Offer priority restaurant reservations, special chef tastings, and birthday celebration perks. Fun mobile app rewards: check-in badges, free movie popcorn points, and gaming discounts.
